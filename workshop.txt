description=[h2]✅ Current Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]🚽 Urination System[/b] — [i]Bladder mechanics with pee needs and puddles[/i]
description=[*][b]💩 Defecation System[/b] — [i]Bowel mechanics with poop needs and feces[/i]
description=
description=[*][b]🎭 Moodles[/b] — [i]Urge indicators and soiled clothing effects.[/i]
description=[*][b]🧬 Traits[/b] — [i]Incontinence, shyness, bed accidents and more. Can be toggled in sandbox settings.[/i]
description=[*][b]📊 Excretion Health GUI[/b] — [i]Track bladder and bowel urgency.[/i]
description=[*][b]🚽 Waste Options[/b] — [i]Toilets, urinals, outhouses, open ground, showers, bathtubs, sinks, bushes, trash cans, dumpsters, water sources, or containers like bottles and jars.[/i]
description=[*][b]👕 Clothing Effects[/b] — [i]Textures, moodles, and penalties for having accidents.[/i]
description=
description=[*][b]💦 Urine Puddles & 💩 Feces[/b] — [i]Physical impacts of your bodily functions[/i]
description=[*][b]⚠️ Urgency Hiccups[/b] — [i]Random animations, sounds, and accident chance when urge is high[/i]
description=[*][b]🧻 Wiping System[/b] — [i]Optional, skipping causes dirtiness[/i]
description=[*][b]🚿 Toilet Water & Flushing[/b] — [i]Flushing toilets need water in the tank. Can be turned off in sandbox settings.[/i]
description=[*][b]🧼 Washing[/b] — [i]Wash soiled clothes, rags and towels at a water source or in a washing machine.[/i]

description=[h2]😲How to Use[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Check Needs[/b]: Open player menu > [b]Excretion[/b] tab.
description=[*][b]Relieve Yourself[/b]: Right-click near valid surfaces.
description=[*][b]Watch for Urges[/b]: Moodles and animations give warnings. Jog to cancel.
description=[*][b]Cleaning Up[/b]:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Water[/b]: Halves dirtiness each time you wash.
description=[*][b]Soap, bleach or cleaning liquid + water[/b]: Removes it in one wash.
description=[*][b]Urine Puddles[/b]: Clean with a mop, toilet brush, dish towel or bath towel.
