ℹ️ How to Use

- **Check Needs**: Open player menu > **Excretion** tab.  
    
- **Relieve Yourself**: Right-click near valid surfaces.  
    
- **Watch for Urges**: Moodles and animations give warnings. Jog to cancel.  
    
- **Cleaning Up**:  
    - **Pee**: Water only.  
        
    - **Poop**: Soap + water.
